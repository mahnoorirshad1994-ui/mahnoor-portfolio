About

I have spent six years building products that people use at scale, from consumer apps with millions of users to AI tools that changed how enterprise teams work day to day. Most of that time was at Bayut and dubizzle, one of the Middle East's leading digital marketplaces, where I worked across the full product lifecycle in both B2C and B2B contexts.

What I find most interesting is the space where user behaviour meets commercial outcomes. The best product decisions I have made have come from understanding not just what users do, but why, and then connecting that to what the business actually needs.

I am currently completing an MBA at INSEAD. Outside of work, I co-founded a learning centre in Pakistan and have taught English and computer skills to migrant workers in the UAE. I care about building products that create access and opportunity for people who do not have enough of either. That is the direction I am building toward, though I am open to where that takes me.
